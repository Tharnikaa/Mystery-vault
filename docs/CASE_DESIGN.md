# MysteryVault — Four Original Case Storylines & Specifications

**Design Principle:** All cases feature original fictional characters, authentic forensic logic, internally consistent timelines, verifiable alibis, genuine contradictions, and unique, objectively testable solutions.

---

## Case 101: The Missing Diamond
- **Setting:** The Grand Elysium Museum of Antiquities, Private Evening Gala.
- **Incident:** At 09:30 PM, the 45-carat *Eye of Orion* diamond disappeared from its display case during a scheduled 3-minute lighting blackout.
- **Suspects:**
  1. `SUS_101_1` — **Julian Drake** (Museum Curator): Claimed he was on the North Mezzanine checking lighting circuits. Truth: Was seen near the vault service stairs.
  2. `SUS_101_2` — **Evelyn Sterling** (Wealthy Gem Collector): Claimed she was speaking with the ambassador by the fountain. Has heavy gambling debts.
  3. `SUS_101_3` — **Marcus Vance** (Head of Security): Holds master override security card. Disabled laser grid citing "sensor calibration".
  4. `SUS_101_4` — **Lila Moreau** (Lead Lighting Technician): Manipulated the circuit breaker at 09:28 PM.
  5. `SUS_101_5` — **Arthur Pendelton** (Museum Janitor): Found duplicate velvet display lining in the maintenance closet.
- **Canonical Culprit:** `SUS_101_3` (Marcus Vance).
- **Motive:** Selling to private black market broker to settle loan shark contracts.
- **Decisive Evidence:** `EVD_101_SEC_LOG` (Security Access Log showing Vance's RFID master card in the vault at 09:29 PM) and `EVD_101_GLASS_CUTTER` (High-grade diamond cutter engraved with Vance's initials found in HVAC vent).
- **Contradiction:** Vance claimed he was monitoring the central camera feeds from the control booth between 09:25 PM and 09:35 PM, but camera logs confirm the booth was empty and his RFID card badged the vault at 09:29 PM.

## Case 102: The Library Code
- **Setting:** St. Jude University Historical Archives & Rare Books Vault.
- **Incident:** The 16th-century *Novum Crypta* cipher manuscript was stolen from the climate-controlled vault minutes before its scheduled donation ceremony.
- **Suspects:**
  1. `SUS_102_1` — **Dr. Harrison Vance** (Senior Archival Professor): Feared the manuscript would disprove his life's academic thesis.
  2. `SUS_102_2` — **Elena Rostova** (Visiting Cryptography Scholar): Discovered an encrypted watermark in the manuscript.
  3. `SUS_102_3` — **Simon Finch** (Graduate Library Assistant): Had access to the vault keys and gloves.
  4. `SUS_102_4` — **Cordelia Cross** (University Trustee & Benefactor): Wanted the manuscript preserved in her private collection.
  5. `SUS_102_5` — **Tobias Reed** (Head Archivist): Signed off on the inventory inspection.
- **Canonical Culprit:** `SUS_102_1` (Dr. Harrison Vance).
- **Motive:** Desperation to suppress historical marginalia that proved his career-defining monograph was plagiarized.
- **Decisive Evidence:** `EVD_102_SPECTACLE_FIBER` (Rare green tweed fiber caught in the vault latch matching Vance's jacket) and `EVD_102_BURNED_PAGE` (Fragments of manuscript margin found in Vance's faculty office hearth).
- **Contradiction:** Dr. Vance claimed he was lecturing students in Hall C from 04:00 PM to 05:30 PM, but student attendance logs and hall CCTV show the lecture ended at 04:20 PM and Vance exited toward the basement archive.

## Case 103: The Poisoned Drink
- **Setting:** Ravenswood Manor, Private Dinner Party.
- **Incident:** Lord Alistair Croft collapsed at 08:45 PM after sipping his customized vintage port. Analysis reveals a fast-acting synthetic sedative causing sudden unconsciousness (strictly non-instructional).
- **Suspects:**
  1. `SUS_103_1` — **Damian Croft** (The Estranged Heir): Deeply in debt, cut out of the family testament.
  2. `SUS_103_2` — **Dr. Eleanor Vance** (Personal Physician): Prescribed medications; had access to medical vials.
  3. `SUS_103_3` — **Vivienne Sinclair** (The Business Partner): Facing an audit Lord Alistair ordered that afternoon.
  4. `SUS_103_4` — **Gerald Finch** (The Butler): Poured and served the wine decanter.
  5. `SUS_103_5` — **Beatrice Croft** (The Grieving Niece): Beneficiary of Croft's private art estate.
- **Canonical Culprit:** `SUS_103_3` (Vivienne Sinclair).
- **Motive:** Lord Croft had discovered Vivienne embezzled £1.2M from their joint holding company and planned to notify authorities the next morning.
- **Decisive Evidence:** `EVD_103_COCKTAIL_GLASS` (Sedative residue found specifically on the inner rim of Lord Croft's personalized crystal glass) and `EVD_103_POWDER_COMPACT` (Compact found in Vivienne's clutch bag with traces of the identical compound).
- **Contradiction:** Vivienne claimed she remained seated at the dining table with Beatrice throughout the 08:30–08:45 PM interval, but Beatrice testified Vivienne slipped into the pantry "to freshen up" at 08:38 PM right before drinks were served.

## Case 104: The Vanishing Prototype
- **Setting:** Apex Dynamics High-Tech Robotics Laboratory.
- **Incident:** The revolutionary *Quantum-Flux Core* prototype disappeared from the cleanroom test bench between 06:15 PM and 06:45 PM before an investor demonstration.
- **Suspects:**
  1. `SUS_104_1` — **Dr. Aris Thorne** (Chief Science Officer): Disagreed with patent ownership terms.
  2. `SUS_104_2` — **Kira Patel** (Firmware Engineer): Recently received competitive recruitment offers.
  3. `SUS_104_3` — **Nolan Vance** (Lab Safety Director): Controls hazardous containment lockers.
  4. `SUS_104_4` — **Maya Lin** (Lead Robotics Hardware Engineer): Designed the magnetic chassis for the prototype.
  5. `SUS_104_5` — **Victor Sterling** (Apex Dynamics CEO): Pressured to show a working product or face bankruptcy.
- **Canonical Culprit:** `SUS_104_2` (Kira Patel).
- **Motive:** Bribed with equity and an executive role by rival syndicate TitanCorp in exchange for the core design and physical sample.
- **Decisive Evidence:** `EVD_104_FARADAY_BAG` (Anti-static RF shielding bag stamped with Kira's workstation serial in the server rack) and `EVD_104_FLASH_DRIVE` (Encrypted USB drive containing extracted core telemetry recovered from Kira's car).
- **Contradiction:** Kira claimed she was in the cafeteria debugging firmware without cleanroom access, but her cleanroom airlock badge timestamp records entry at 06:22 PM and exit at 06:38 PM.
